Arduino Claw

An autonomous object-handling system using ultrasonic sensing.

Project

Designed an autonomous claw with closed-loop actuation logic driven by ultrasonic distance measurements.

Control focus

Calibrated the sensors to reliably detect objects and trigger actuation at the right moment, turning distance measurements into predictable mechanical behavior.
