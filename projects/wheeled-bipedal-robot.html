Personal Project

Wheeled Bipedal Robot

A self-balancing robotic platform with custom mechanical and control design.

Mechanical design

Designed the structure in SolidWorks using sheet metal and 3D printed components, balancing low weight against the rigidity needed for dynamic motion.

Control challenge

Integrated BLDC motors with FOC control for two-wheel balancing. The key design trade-off was reducing weight while maintaining performance without relying on oversized, expensive motors.
