FretNot

A beginner-friendly guitar gadget that lights up chord positions in real time.

Project

Built by a team of four in under 24 hours. FretNot displays the location of song chords using laser diodes driven by an ESP32 and paired over Bluetooth with a web browser front end.

My contribution

I worked across hardware and firmware integration, connecting the electronics to the interactive browser experience and troubleshooting the system under tight time pressure.
