About / Mission

Building intelligent and practical machines

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at the University of British Columbia, pursuing the Mechatronics Option. I'm interested in control theory and mechanical design, and my goal is to develop robots capable of performing human tasks to enable real-world automation.


I work across the full stack of building a machine — from CAD modeling and structural design, to embedded firmware, motor control, and experimental iteration. I've shipped robotics subsystems for autonomous soccer-playing robots with UBC Thunderbots, and I'm currently a Mechanical Designer Co-op at IMW Industries.
